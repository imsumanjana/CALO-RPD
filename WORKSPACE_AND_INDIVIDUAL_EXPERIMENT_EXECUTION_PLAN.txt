CALO-RPD STUDIO
WORKSPACE-MANAGED STUDY CAMPAIGNS AND INDIVIDUAL EXPERIMENT EXECUTION PLAN
Planning date: 2026-08-16
Status: IMPLEMENTED IN SOURCE - OWNER VALIDATION PENDING
Revision: Immutable Workspace and individual plans with exclusive durable controller

===============================================================================
1. PURPOSE AND USER INTENT
===============================================================================

This plan defines two scientist-controlled experiment paths after the scientist
submits an algorithm stage:

1. Workspace-managed portfolio/study execution
   - The scientist creates a portfolio or multi-experiment study.
   - The study may use all staged algorithms or an explicit subset of them.
   - The Workspace owns staging, queue construction, execution, safe pause,
     exact resume, and completion of every experiment in that study.
   - Once the Workspace campaign is staged, the Experiment tab is frozen while
     Workspace work is staged, running, or still pausing.
   - After the Workspace campaign reaches a durably committed safe PAUSED state,
     or is durably closed in a terminal state, individual experiments are
     enabled. Workspace and individual execution are never concurrent.

2. Experiment-managed individual custom execution
   - The scientist creates and runs one customized experiment directly from the
     Experiment tab.
   - By default, the individual experiment consumes the complete algorithm set
     submitted on the Algorithms tab.
   - It cannot silently add, replace, or remove staged algorithms.
   - If active execution is being directed by a Workspace campaign, the
     Experiment tab is read-only/frozen and the Workspace-supplied experiment
     snapshot is used. A safely paused Workspace campaign is retained but does
     not prevent a new individual experiment.

Terminology used in this plan:

- "Workspace" means the application's Workspace ribbon tab. The word
  "workshop" in the request is treated as Workspace.
- "Algorithm staged" means the scientist pressed "Submit algorithms for
  experiment" and the stage was accepted. Merely checking a box is still a
  draft and does not unlock these workflows.
- "Staged algorithm pool" is the complete immutable set of algorithm identities
  and parameter snapshots submitted on the Algorithms tab.
- "Study filter" is a non-empty subset of the staged algorithm pool selected for
  one Workspace portfolio/study. It is not a new algorithm stage.
- "Execution controller" is the exclusive authority currently allowed to stage,
  launch, pause, resume, or cancel experiment work: NONE, WORKSPACE, or
  INDIVIDUAL_EXPERIMENT.

The scientist authorized source implementation on 2026-08-16. Implementation was
strictly limited to this plan. No test, validator, experiment, policy workflow,
qualification, protected-case workflow, Docker operation, or release action was
executed while implementing it.

2. NON-NEGOTIABLE RULES
===============================================================================

2.1 Algorithm-stage authority

- Workspace and Experiment navigation become available only after a valid,
  non-empty algorithm stage has been submitted.
- Draft checkbox changes do not alter an existing stage.
- The staged algorithm pool is the upper bound for every study and experiment.
- A Workspace study may select:
    study_algorithms = any non-empty subset of staged_algorithms
- It must always be true that:
    set(study_algorithms) <= set(staged_algorithms)
- An individual custom experiment uses the complete staged algorithm pool by
  default. It does not expose a second algorithm selector.
- A Workspace-generated experiment uses the exact subset bound into that
  Workspace study item.
- No portfolio/article preset may write directly to the authoritative staged
  algorithm list.
- If a preset requires an algorithm that is not staged, staging the Workspace
  plan must fail closed and show the exact missing identities. The message must
  direct the scientist back to Algorithms to reset/select/submit a broader pool.
- No code may silently import algorithms from the executable registry, replace
  the staged set with a preset, or interpret an unavailable algorithm as staged.

2.2 Exclusive execution ownership

- At most one execution controller may own mutable experiment state or execute
  numerical work at a time.
- When controller = WORKSPACE and the Workspace plan state is STAGED, RUNNING,
  PAUSING, or INTERRUPTED_RESUMABLE:
    - the complete Experiment ribbon category is disabled;
    - direct individual staging and launch actions are disabled;
    - an already visible Experiment page becomes read-only and displays the
      Workspace campaign identity and a button to return to Workspace > Study;
    - Experiment cannot alter case, formulation, scenarios, runs, budget,
      algorithms, seeds, compute intent, or output location.
- PAUSING is not PAUSED. Experiment remains frozen until active Workspace work
  has reached its declared safe boundary and the PAUSED state, checkpoints,
  task ledger, and controller release have been committed atomically.
- When a Workspace campaign is durably PAUSED:
    - controller = NONE unless an individual experiment subsequently acquires it;
    - the Experiment tab is enabled for individual setup, staging, and execution;
    - the paused Workspace plan, queue, completed evidence, and checkpoints stay
      immutable and resumable;
    - Algorithms Submit/Reset remains blocked so the stage bound to the paused
      Workspace campaign cannot be invalidated;
    - Workspace Resume is enabled only while controller = NONE, and is disabled
      while an individual experiment is staged, running, pausing, paused, or
      interrupted-resumable.
- A "closed Workspace campaign" means a durably committed terminal campaign
  state (COMPLETED, COMPLETED_WITH_FAILURES, CANCELLED, or
  FAILED_NON_RESUMABLE), not merely closing a page or application window. A
  running campaign cannot release authority because its page was closed.
- When controller = INDIVIDUAL_EXPERIMENT and the individual plan is STAGED,
  RUNNING, PAUSING, PAUSED, or INTERRUPTED_RESUMABLE:
    - Workspace planning pages may remain inspectable;
    - Workspace campaign Stage/Run/Resume controls are disabled;
    - Workspace cannot replace the active individual configuration.
- This symmetric safety rule prevents two pages from controlling the same
  ExperimentConfig and ExperimentManager concurrently.
- Completed evidence is never deleted or rewritten when controller ownership is
  released.

2.3 Policy and scientific boundaries

- Rule-based CALO remains usable without policy training.
- TSH-CALO is executable only when its separately qualified, explicitly
  selected, activated, immutable, checksum-valid policy binding is ready.
- A study filter cannot bypass TSH-CALO policy gating.
- Workspace planning cannot train, qualify, select, activate, or modify a policy.
- Every planned comparison preserves exact function-evaluation accounting,
  paired seeds where required, common case/formulation/scenario semantics, and
  the existing fairness audit.
- Protected cases remain outside training, tuning, reward design, and checkpoint
  selection.
- Pause/resume changes operational state only; it cannot alter the frozen study
  design, algorithm pool, filtered subset, seeds, budgets, or policy binding.

3. PROPOSED RIBBON OWNERSHIP
===============================================================================

The ribbon remains duplicate-free. High-frequency actions belong inside the
opened page rather than being repeated as ribbon buttons.

3.1 Algorithms tab

Ribbon buttons:

1. Algorithms
   - Opens algorithm selection and staging.
   - Scientist selects the broad pool that may be used by later studies.

2. CALO Intelligence
   - Opens governing CALO/TSH-CALO readiness and policy lifecycle evidence.

3. CALO Settings
   - Opens rule-based CALO and permitted TSH-CALO runtime/search settings.

Page actions retained:

- Submit algorithms for experiment
- Reset selection

Additional planned stage display:

- Stage identity/fingerprint
- Staged algorithm count and names
- "Used by Workspace campaign <id>" or "Used by individual plan <id>" when
  ownership is active
- Reset/Submit disabled while either controller owns work or a resumable paused
  Workspace campaign remains bound to the stage; the scientist must first
  discard an unstarted plan or finish/cancel/close the bound campaign according
  to its lifecycle rules

3.2 Workspace tab

Workspace ribbon buttons remain:

1. Portfolio
2. Study
3. Validate
4. Bench
5. Export
6. Settings

Their roles are:

PORTFOLIO
- Defines evidence intent, portfolio kind, requested outputs, minimum repeated
  runs, storage, result reuse, validation needs, and study strength.
- Provides the study-algorithm filter, but only over the staged algorithm pool.
- Never launches work directly.

STUDY
- Owns Workspace campaign review, staging, multi-experiment queue generation,
  execution, progress, safe pause, exact resume, and remaining-work management.
- It is the only Workspace page that acquires/relinquishes WORKSPACE execution
  authority.

VALIDATE
- Validates retained completed results selected by the Workspace campaign.
- Does not stage or launch numerical optimization.

BENCH
- Configures/inspects benchmark blocks used by a Workspace study.
- A benchmark plan may create multiple study cells but cannot expand the staged
  algorithm pool.

EXPORT
- Generates only portfolio-requested artifacts from eligible retained evidence.
- Does not change the campaign, algorithm subset, or scientific plan.

SETTINGS
- Controls application-level appearance, density, and paths.
- It does not own scientific execution settings and cannot change a staged plan.

3.3 Experiment tab

Proposed Experiment ribbon buttons:

1. Individual experiment (primary)
   - Opens the complete direct custom-experiment surface.
   - Uses the full submitted algorithm pool when Workspace is not controlling.
   - Shows a frozen Workspace-owned explanation when controller = WORKSPACE.

2. Power system
   - Opens case selection, base power flow, and independent cross-check inputs.

3. ORPD formulation
   - Opens objectives, variables, decoding, constraints, and tolerances.

4. Robust scenarios
   - Opens deterministic/robust scenario and aggregation configuration.

No Portfolio, Workspace Study, Validate, Bench, Export, Results, Live, Stats, or
Settings buttons are added to Experiment. Cancel/pause/resume are page-local and
mode-specific so ribbon tabs do not repeat the same commands.

4. WORKSPACE PAGE BUTTON PLAN
===============================================================================

4.1 Workspace > Portfolio page

Section A - Staged algorithm pool (read-only)

- Displays stage ID, fingerprint, algorithm identities, parameter fingerprints,
  and governing-policy binding summary where applicable.
- Rows have "Include in this study" checkboxes.
- Rows not in the staged pool do not appear as selectable options.
- Registry algorithms outside the stage may be shown only in a separate
  read-only "Not staged" information list, never as enabled checkboxes.

Buttons:

1. Use all staged algorithms
   - Checks every algorithm in the staged pool for this study draft.
   - Does not modify the Algorithms stage.

2. Clear study filter
   - Clears only the Workspace draft subset.
   - Does not discard or alter the global staged pool.

3. Apply selected study subset
   - Saves the non-empty filtered subset into the Workspace draft.
   - Validates subset containment and applicable policy readiness.

4. Preview portfolio requirements
   - Computes required runs, fields, validation, statistics, benchmark blocks,
     storage, and disabled outputs without mutating the stage or launching work.

5. Save Workspace draft
   - Saves portfolio settings and the filtered subset as an editable draft.
   - Does not acquire execution authority and does not freeze Experiment.

Preset behavior:

- Presets provide required/recommended algorithm identities.
- Required identity missing from staged pool:
    BLOCK: "This study requires <names>. Return to Algorithms and submit a
    broader staged pool first."
- Recommended identity missing:
    show the missing identity and the evidence limitation;
    never add it automatically.
- The scientist may proceed without a recommendation only if all formal minimum
  algorithm/evidence requirements remain satisfied and the limitation is
  explicit.

4.2 Workspace > Study page

This is the Workspace execution authority surface.

Summary blocks:

- Staged algorithm pool
- Applied study subset
- Portfolio/study type
- Case/formulation/scenario matrix
- Runs, seeds, budgets, compute intent, output root
- Total experiment cells and total algorithm-run jobs
- Reusable/completed/new job counts
- Frozen plan fingerprint
- Current controller and lifecycle state

Buttons before Workspace staging:

1. Review study matrix
   - Expands the draft into explicit experiment cells and job counts.
   - No database campaign and no execution authority are created.

2. Run Workspace fairness audit
   - Audits subset containment, policy gates, cases, scenarios, paired seeds,
     budgets, output requirements, reuse compatibility, and compute readiness.
   - Scientific checks remain explicit and backgrounded as today.

3. Stage Workspace campaign
   - Available only after review and a passing audit for the unchanged draft.
   - Atomically persists the frozen Workspace plan and its queue.
   - Acquires controller = WORKSPACE.
   - Changes Workspace lifecycle DRAFT -> STAGED.
   - Freezes the Experiment tab immediately.
   - Does not start numerical execution.

4. Discard Workspace draft
   - Clears only an un-staged draft.
   - Does not affect the global staged algorithm pool.

Buttons after Workspace staging:

5. Run campaign
   - Starts all unfinished experiment cells in the frozen Workspace queue.
   - May run sequentially or with the permitted worker/resource schedule.
   - Does not change the plan when execution begins.

6. Pause campaign safely
   - Stops admission of new jobs.
   - Allows active jobs to reach the existing atomic safe boundary.
   - Commits completed evidence and resumable checkpoints.
   - Transitions RUNNING -> PAUSING -> PAUSED.
   - Keeps Experiment frozen during PAUSING.
   - Atomically releases WORKSPACE authority only after PAUSED is durably
     committed, then enables individual Experiment work.

7. Resume campaign
   - Enabled only for one exact authenticated paused/interrupted Workspace
     campaign.
   - For PAUSED, requires controller = NONE and atomically reacquires WORKSPACE
     authority before any queue or campaign state is mutated.
   - Remains disabled while an individual experiment owns the controller.
   - Re-verifies plan fingerprint, source/provenance, stage fingerprint,
     algorithm subset, parameter snapshots, policy binding, task ledger, and
     checkpoints.
   - Executes unfinished work only.

8. Cancel remaining campaign
   - Explicit destructive-to-the-queue action with confirmation.
   - Retains completed results and provenance.
   - Marks unfinished queued items cancelled; it never rewrites completed data.
   - Releases Workspace authority only after active work has stopped safely.

9. Discard staged Workspace campaign
   - Allowed only while STAGED and never started, with explicit confirmation.
   - Removes the mutable staging/queue record, not completed evidence.
   - Releases controller = WORKSPACE and unfreezes Experiment.

10. Open campaign queue
    - Shows each experiment cell, algorithm-run jobs, assigned/reused/completed/
      paused/failed status, exact progress, and retained identifiers.

11. Open Live Optimization
    - Navigates to Compute > Live Optimization.
    - Navigation does not change controller ownership or execution state.

Multi-experiment behavior:

- A Workspace study expands deterministically into experiment cells, for
  example:
    cases x formulations x scenario blocks x study subsets x independent runs
- Each cell receives an immutable config snapshot and fingerprint.
- All jobs retain the parent Workspace plan ID and algorithm-stage ID.
- Scheduling may use sequential or safe parallel lanes, but fairness, exact FE
  accounting, device admission, and deterministic seeds remain unchanged.
- A completed cell is never recomputed unless the scientist explicitly creates
  a new plan/revision; compatible result reuse remains fingerprint-gated.

5. INDIVIDUAL EXPERIMENT PAGE BUTTON PLAN
===============================================================================

Experiment > Individual experiment is the direct customized path.

Configuration behavior:

- Scientist customizes one case/formulation/scenario/run/budget/seed/compute
  configuration.
- The algorithm list is read-only and equals the complete staged algorithm pool.
- There is no second algorithm checkbox/filter list on this page.
- If the scientist needs more or fewer algorithms for direct execution, they
  must return to Algorithms and submit a new global stage before staging the
  individual experiment.
- When the page is opened by Workspace direction for an active study cell, it
  displays the Workspace-supplied immutable subset/config snapshot read-only;
  the Experiment tab itself remains frozen as an execution controller.

Buttons when controller = NONE:

1. Save individual setup
   - Saves the custom draft only.
   - Does not start work or acquire execution authority.

2. Run individual fairness audit
   - Audits the exact current custom draft and full staged algorithm pool.

3. Stage individual experiment
   - Requires a passing audit for the unchanged draft.
   - Persists one immutable individual plan.
   - Acquires controller = INDIVIDUAL_EXPERIMENT.
   - Does not start execution.

4. Run individual experiment
   - Starts the staged individual plan using the shared ExperimentManager.

5. Pause individual safely
   - Uses the same atomic operational pause semantics as a Workspace campaign.

6. Resume individual experiment
   - Re-verifies the exact individual plan/stage/checkpoint bindings and resumes
   unfinished jobs only.

7. Cancel individual experiment
   - Stops/cancels according to the existing safe task contract and retains
   completed evidence.

8. Discard individual staging
   - Available only before execution begins, with confirmation.
   - Releases INDIVIDUAL_EXPERIMENT authority.

Behavior when controller = WORKSPACE:

- Every editable control and action above is disabled.
- Page banner:
  "Workspace campaign <id> controls experiment execution. Manage staging, run,
  pause, resume, and queue actions from Workspace > Study."
- Available read-only buttons:
    View Workspace plan
    View current Workspace job
    Return to Workspace Study
- No hidden shortcut, keyboard action, context-pane action, or restored window
  state may bypass the freeze.

Behavior when a Workspace campaign is durably PAUSED and controller = NONE:

- The individual Experiment controls are enabled.
- A banner identifies the paused Workspace campaign and warns that staging an
  individual experiment temporarily disables Workspace Resume.
- The individual experiment still uses the complete unchanged staged algorithm
  pool; it does not inherit the Workspace study subset.
- Opening, staging, or running the individual experiment does not modify the
  paused Workspace plan, queue, evidence, or checkpoints.

6. EXECUTION-CONTROLLER STATE MACHINE
===============================================================================

Controller values:

- NONE
- WORKSPACE
- INDIVIDUAL_EXPERIMENT

Plan lifecycle values:

- EMPTY
- DRAFT
- AUDITED
- STAGED
- RUNNING
- PAUSING
- PAUSED
- INTERRUPTED_RESUMABLE
- COMPLETED
- COMPLETED_WITH_FAILURES
- CANCELLED
- FAILED_NON_RESUMABLE

Key transitions:

NONE + valid algorithm stage
  -> Workspace and Experiment navigation enabled

Workspace draft saved
  -> controller remains NONE
  -> Experiment remains enabled

Workspace campaign staged
  -> controller = WORKSPACE
  -> Workspace lifecycle = STAGED
  -> Experiment tab frozen

Workspace Run campaign
  -> STAGED -> RUNNING while controller remains WORKSPACE

Workspace Pause safely
  -> RUNNING -> PAUSING while controller remains WORKSPACE
  -> Experiment remains frozen throughout PAUSING
  -> after atomic safe-pause commit: PAUSING -> PAUSED and controller = NONE
  -> Experiment becomes enabled for individual work

Workspace Resume
  -> PAUSED + controller NONE -> atomically acquire WORKSPACE
  -> PAUSED -> RUNNING after exact verification
  -> disabled while controller = INDIVIDUAL_EXPERIMENT

Individual experiment staged while Workspace is PAUSED
  -> paused Workspace lifecycle and artifacts remain unchanged
  -> controller = INDIVIDUAL_EXPERIMENT
  -> Workspace Resume freezes

Individual terminal completion/cancellation or unstarted discard while a
Workspace campaign is PAUSED
  -> controller = NONE
  -> Workspace Resume becomes available again

Workspace terminal completion/cancellation
  -> lifecycle terminal
  -> controller = NONE after durable final status commit
  -> Experiment unfreezes

Workspace unstarted stage discarded
  -> STAGED -> EMPTY or DRAFT
  -> controller = NONE
  -> Experiment unfreezes

Individual experiment staged
  -> controller = INDIVIDUAL_EXPERIMENT
  -> Workspace Stage/Run controls freeze

Individual terminal completion/cancellation or unstarted discard
  -> controller = NONE
  -> Workspace Stage/Run controls unfreeze

Algorithm stage reset request while controller != NONE
  -> blocked with exact controlling plan/campaign identity
  -> no automatic cancellation or invalidation

Application relaunch with retained STAGED/RUNNING/PAUSING/PAUSED state
  -> restore WORKSPACE controller for authenticated STAGED/RUNNING/PAUSING or
     resumable interrupted active work
  -> restore controller NONE for a durably committed PAUSED Workspace campaign
     unless an authenticated individual controller record is active
  -> in-flight status becomes INTERRUPTED_RESUMABLE only when the existing
     campaign/checkpoint contracts prove resumability
  -> the correct freeze or paused handoff state is restored before edits

- lifecycle state

- schema_version

===============================================================================
10. FAILURE, PAUSE, RESUME, AND MULTI-EXPERIMENT RULES
===============================================================================

Safe pause:

- No new job starts after pause is latched.
- Active jobs stop only at their declared safe/atomic boundary.
- Completed results commit once.
- Exact optimizer checkpoint evidence is retained where supported.
- Algorithms without exact in-run resume restart from their original paired seed
  for that unfinished job; they are never presented as exact in-run continuation.
- For Workspace campaigns, PAUSING retains WORKSPACE authority and keeps
  Experiment frozen.
- Only the atomic PAUSED commit releases WORKSPACE authority and enables an
  individual experiment.

Resume:

- Resume the exact plan/campaign only.
- Verify stage hash, subset, config/cell hashes, task ledger, policy binding,
  source/provenance contract, result reuse, and checkpoints.
- Never merge jobs from a different Workspace or individual plan.
- Never regenerate seeds or budgets.
- A paused Workspace campaign may resume only after any intervening individual
  experiment has completed, been cancelled, or had its unstarted staging
  discarded and controller = NONE.

Multiple experiments:

- Workspace may expand one plan into multiple experiment cells.
- The queue records planned, reused, queued, running, pausing, paused,
  interrupted, completed, failed, and cancelled states per cell/job.
- Failure of one independent cell follows the declared campaign failure policy;
  it does not rewrite successful cells.
- Resume executes only unfinished eligible cells/jobs.
- Queue order and parallel scheduling cannot change scientific identities.

Controller/freeze failures:

- Missing/corrupt controller record fails closed: no launch from either tab.
- UI freeze is derived from persisted authority, not from which page is visible.
- A disabled tab must show the exact owner plan/campaign and recovery action in
  its tooltip/status rather than silently doing nothing.

12. REQUIRED TEST AND FALSIFICATION PLAN
===============================================================================

Algorithm subset invariants:

- Workspace accepts a non-empty exact subset.
- Workspace rejects an algorithm outside the stage.
