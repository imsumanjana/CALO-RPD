# Data instructions

- Treat source data, schemas, frozen records, and trained artifacts according to their deeper instructions.
- Do not overwrite frozen evidence or generated policy artifacts.
- New scientific datasets require source identity, license review, checksums, and role separation.
- During Phase 4, inventory old policy data and prepare path-confined dry-run removal only. Do not
  delete or rewrite it; post-freeze removal requires explicit authorization and a deletion receipt.
