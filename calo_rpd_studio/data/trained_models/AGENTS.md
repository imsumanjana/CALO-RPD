# Trained-model instructions

- Treat checked-in model records as immutable historical artifacts.
- Do not train into, rewrite, re-sign, auto-activate, or reinterpret these artifacts.
- New policy candidates and runtime checkpoints must remain outside source control.
- Existing models are development-only, unqualified, inactive, non-final, and excluded from release
  and new-policy initialization.
- Preserve them byte-for-byte during Phase 4. A separately authorized post-freeze removal may delete
  only exact inventoried targets after dependent registrations are cleared and a receipt is retained.
